
The article titled "Harnessing neural variability: Implications for brain research and non-invasive brain stimulation" pulled my attention due to the possible extensive application of neural variability in the scope of brain stimulation. This area is a place where we can take active role in making experiments associated with working brain. We know that the brain is highly variable itself alone and stimulating it leads to more unexpected outcomes with the amplificaiton of inherent neural variability.

This article highlights the important distinction between neuronal noise and variability which closely interacted with each other. At the neuronal level, generation of spikes depends on two main factors;

- Internal state of the neuron
- External input through synaptic transmission

However, it is still ambigous whether single neuron will generate spike even if the external input and internal state are exactly known.
